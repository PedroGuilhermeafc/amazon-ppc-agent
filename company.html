Avalantis Comercio Digital LTDA

Avalantis Comercio Digital LTDA is a Brazilian e-commerce company engaged in the sale of consumer products through online marketplaces, including Amazon.

Company Information

Legal name: Avalantis Comercio Digital LTDA

Country of incorporation: Brazil

Advertising Technology

The company develops and uses internal technology to assist with the analysis, management, and optimization of its own digital advertising campaigns.

This includes an internal advertising optimization project intended to support campaign performance analysis, reporting, keyword and targeting analysis, bid and budget optimization, and other advertising management activities for the company's own advertising accounts.

Amazon Ads API

Avalantis Comercio Digital LTDA intends to use the Amazon Ads API solely to access, analyze, and manage advertising data and campaigns associated with its own Amazon advertising account.
