Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Amazon PPC Agent is an internal application designed to assist with the analysis, optimization, and management of Amazon Ads advertising campaigns.

Information Accessed

When authorized by the Amazon Ads account owner, the application may access advertising-related information made available through the Amazon Ads API, including campaign data, keywords, targeting, search terms, bids, budgets, performance metrics, and advertising reports.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to analyze advertising performance, generate recommendations, assist with campaign optimization, and perform advertising management actions authorized by the account owner.

Data Sharing

Amazon PPC Agent is intended for internal use. Advertising account data is not sold. Data may be processed by technology service providers required to operate the application, subject to their applicable privacy and security terms.

Authorization

Access to Amazon Ads data requires authorization from the Amazon Ads account owner through Amazon's authentication and authorization mechanisms.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and advertising information from unauthorized access. Authentication credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and client secrets are not intended to be publicly exposed.

Data Retention

Advertising information may be retained only as necessary to provide campaign analysis, maintain optimization history, and evaluate the results of advertising changes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Amazon PPC Agent evolves or as integrations and data-processing practices change.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the project's GitHub repository: Amazon PPC Agent .
